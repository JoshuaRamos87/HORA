HORA is a quiet, minimalist personal gallery designed for unhurried looking. A new artwork arrives cleanly at the top of each hour, with up to 5 skips per hour and instant filter previews.

Artworks and metadata are fetched directly in your browser from two open museum collections:

Art Institute of Chicago via api.artic.edu (collection data CC0; curatorial descriptions CC BY 4.0).

The Metropolitan Museum of Art via collectionapi.metmuseum.org (Open Access CC0), supplemented with short historical notes from Wikipedia/Wikidata when linked.
